## B 站 · 中文

### 标题

基于马尔科夫链的生成式音乐 | Audiovisual | 30分钟 · 专注与思考

### 简介

完全的随机毫无意义，生成式音乐需要系统和规则。

以已有的音符、节奏和乐句为素材，马尔科夫链根据当前音高与转移概率选择后续音符，让音乐在推进中不断产生变化，同时保留原有的节奏与段落结构。

作品既可以被观看和聆听，也可以作为空间中持续运行的背景影像与音乐。

## YouTube · English

### Title

Markov Chain Generative Music | Audiovisual | 30 Minutes · Focus & Reflection

### Description

Pure randomness is meaningless. Generative music needs systems and rules.

Using existing notes, rhythms, and phrases as material, a Markov chain selects subsequent notes based on the current pitch and transition probabilities. The music continually changes as it unfolds, while preserving the original rhythms and sectional structure.

The work can be watched and listened to, or played continuously as background visuals and music in a space.
